# AdventureWorks Financial Performance Analysis

## Executive Summary

AdventureWorks needed to understand which international markets generated the strongest financial results and whether marketing investment was being allocated efficiently.

I used SQL to integrate sales, product, customer, territory, cost, and marketing campaign data. I then summarized the results in Excel to compare revenue, gross profit, margin, and marketing ROI across six countries.

The analysis identified $9.78 million in revenue, $4.19 million in gross profit, and a global margin of 42.88%. The United States produced the strongest marketing return, with a 75.75% ROI, making it the most attractive market for prioritizing future investment.

## Business Problem

The project focused on three business questions:

- Which countries generate the highest revenue and gross profit?
- Which markets operate with the strongest margins?
- Is marketing investment being allocated to the countries with the highest returns?

The objective was not only to identify the markets with the greatest sales volume, but also to determine whether those markets were converting marketing investment into profitable growth.
